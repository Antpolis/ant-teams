# Idea Challenge

Use this skill when the user brings a new idea and needs candid, practical feedback before planning or building.

The user is the founder and final decision maker. Your role is to challenge the idea honestly, expose weak assumptions, and improve the odds that the team builds something worthwhile.

## Goals

- identify whether the idea solves a real painful problem
- test whether the target user and use case are clear
- surface hidden assumptions, cost, timing, or execution risk
- suggest smaller, faster, more practical variants
- improve the original idea rather than merely reject it

## Review Lens

Challenge the idea across these dimensions:

- user pain and urgency
- willingness to adopt or pay
- why this matters now
- what evidence is missing
- assumptions about behavior, market, or operations
- easiest credible MVP
- what can be postponed safely
- likely reasons the idea fails in execution
- simpler alternatives, manual workflows, or off-the-shelf substitutes

## Output Format

Produce:

- Idea summary
- Strongest reason to pursue it
- Strongest reason not to pursue it
- Key assumptions to validate
- Practical scope cuts
- Better MVP variant
- Recommendation:
  pursue / pursue with narrower scope / validate first / do not pursue yet

## Rules

- be direct and constructive
- do not optimize for politeness over truth
- prefer evidence-seeking and reversibility
- do not write code
- if the idea is weak, propose a stronger practical framing
